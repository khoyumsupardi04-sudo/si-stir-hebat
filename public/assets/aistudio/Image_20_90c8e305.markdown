## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-looking, cheerful, and digitally capable academic identity tailored for an Indonesian primary education environment. The aesthetic balances pedagogical trust with lively, youth-centered joy.

- **Audience & Context:** Primary school educators, young pupils, school administrators, and parents interacting with primary school academic tracking and student growth portals in Boalemo, Gorontalo.
- **Tone:** Energetic (*semangat*), nurturing, approachable, and cleanly structured. It avoids rigid institutional bureaucracy in favor of encouraging, modern digital interfaces.
- **Design Style:** A contemporary fusion of **Soft Neomorphic Minimalism** and **Vibrant Playful Materialism**. Interfaces feature generous rounded forms, tactile pill-like micro-interactions, soft pastel tonal planes, and punchy saturated accents that spark curiosity and engagement while preserving absolute clarity for data entry and reading.
- **Language Default:** All copy, actions, validation patterns, and navigational anchors default to localized, natural Bahasa Indonesia.

## Colors

The palette leverages a rich royal violet core layered against luminous pastels and crisp supportive status hues.

- **Primary Tones:** `#4C1D95` serves as the anchor for structural bars, major brand elements, primary CTAs, and high-emphasis focal nodes. Tinted variations (`#EDE9FE` and `#C4B5FD`) handle secondary container backgrounds, pill highlights, and hover transitions.
- **Luminance & Sky Accents:** `#7DD3FC` and its soft tint `#E0F2FE` deliver breathing room, representing collaborative spaces, student profiles, and interactive badges.
- **Playful Energy:** `#EC4899` with background counterpart `#F9A8D4` functions as a celebration accent—used for achievements, reward badges, notices, and dynamic visual markers.
- **Canvas & Neutral Surfaces:** The base viewport canvas rests on `#FAF9FF`. Card surfaces sit elevated on `#FFFFFF`. High-priority text and typography use `#1F1B2E`, while helper labels and secondary details utilize `#6B6880`.
- **System States:**
  - *Berhasil / Sukses (Success):* `#22C55E`
  - *Peringatan (Warning):* `#F59E0B`
  - *Kendala / Batal (Critical):* `#EF4444`
  - *Informasi (Info):* `#38BDF8`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines the open, friendly geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the dense, functional legibility of **Inter**.

- **Display & Headings:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers warm curves and structural clarity. Headings maintain tighter line-heights and pronounced weights (600–800) to create clear content landmarks for rapid scanning by teachers and students alike.
- **Body & Data:** Inter handles form inputs, student report tables, narrative evaluation paragraphs, and meta descriptions. It ensures maximum readability across varying hardware displays, especially on mobile devices and budget laptops.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Badges, navigation tabs, button titles, and metric counters leverage Plus Jakarta Sans (Semibold to Bold) for punchy execution in compact spaces.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an adaptable 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to accommodate both dense desktop administrative dashboards and touch-first mobile interfaces for parents and field staff.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Mobile (< 768px):` 4-column layout, outer margin `1rem`, gutter `0.75rem`. Cards and table views reflow into vertical summary cards.
  - `Tablet (768px - 1024px):` 8-column layout, outer margin `1.5rem`, gutter `1rem`. Dual-column content zones.
  - `Desktop (> 1024px):` 12-column layout, max content container `1280px`, outer margin `2rem`, gutter `1.25rem`.
- **Rhythm & Flow:** Elements leverage an 8pt base grid logic (`0.25rem`, `0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.25rem`). Component cards prioritize spacious inner paddings (`space-lg` on desktop, `space-md` on mobile) to eliminate cognitive strain during student record reviews.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is produced using delicate violet-tinted atmospheric ambient shadows combined with tonal layering, rather than harsh, generic grey drop shadows.

- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Flat base surface rendered in `#FAF9FF`.
- **Layer 1 (Card Surfaces):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces paired with a soft ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(76, 29, 149, 0.05)`. Rimmed with an optional delicate border `rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.35)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating & Interactive Elevates):** Hovered cards, interactive score items, and floating toolbars use `0 10px 25px -4px rgba(76, 29, 149, 0.09)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Drawers):** High-priority overlays (e.g., student detail sheets, confirm modals) use `0 20px 40px -6px rgba(31, 27, 46, 0.16)` with a backdrop blur `blur(8px)` tinted with `rgba(31, 27, 46, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary prioritizes organic friendliness, soft touchpoints, and approachable rounded corners that feel gentle and inviting to young learners.

- **Cards & Major Panels:** Built with generous corner curvatures spanning `16px` (`rounded-lg`) up to `24px` (`rounded-xl`).
- **Interactive Controls:** Buttons, input shells, dropdown triggers, and status badges utilize consistent `12px` to `16px` boundaries or full pill capsules (`rounded-full`) for chips and indicators.
- **Decorative Accents:** Sub-containers and icon backgrounds feature squircle geometries or circular badges that soften data-heavy metrics.

## Components

### Tombol (Buttons)
- **Primer (Primary):** Solid `#4C1D95` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-xl` (12px), active state features subtle scaling (`transform: scale(0.98)`). Glow focus rings use `rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.5)`.
- **Sekunder (Secondary):** Tinted background `#EDE9FE`, text `#4C1D95`, borderless. Hover shifts to `#C4B5FD` with deepened contrast.
- **Aksen Ceria (Accent / Reward):** Solid `#EC4899`, white text, designated for celebration actions, point awards, or interactive assignments.
- **Garis Luar (Outline):** 1.5px border `#C4B5FD`, transparent fill, `#4C1D95` label text.

### Kartu Siswa & Konten (Cards)
- Solid `#FFFFFF` background, `20px` to `24px` border radius (`rounded-xl`), inner padding `space-lg`.
- Highlight headers feature a soft gradient accent line or pastel banner tint (`#E0F2FE` or `#EDE9FE`).
- Metric cards showcase high-contrast figures set in *Plus Jakarta Sans Bold*, accompanied by miniature iconography encased in squircle pastel containers.

### Kolom Input (Input Fields & Dropdowns)
- Flat surface `#FFFFFF`, bordered by 1.5px `#EDE9FE`.
- Corner curvature at `12px`. Inner padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- Focus state activates an outline border `#4C1D95` and an outer ring of `0 0 0 4px rgba(125, 211, 252, 0.35)`.
- Helper captions and validation error text appear in Inter `body-sm` (`#EF4444` for invalid input, `#22C55E` for valid confirmations).

### Lencana & Filter (Chips & Badges)
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with horizontal padding `0.75rem` and vertical padding `0.25rem`.
- **Status Akademik:**
  - *Lengkap / Aktif:* `#22C55E` background tint at 15%, solid text `#15803D`.
  - *Perlu Perhatian:* `#F59E0B` tint at 15%, solid text `#B45309`.
  - *Tugas Baru:* `#E0F2FE` background, text `#0369A1`.
  - *Prestasi:* `#FCE7F3` background, text `#BE185D`.

### Pilihan Interaktif (Checkboxes & Radio Buttons)
- 20px size with rounded edges (`6px` for checkboxes, circular for radios).
- Inactive state: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1.5px` border `#C4B5FD`.
- Checked state: Filled with primary `#4C1D95` containing a white check or inner dot, snapping into view via an elastic transition.

### Komponen Khas: Papan Prestasi & Rapor Cepat (Quick Report Widgets)
- Compact progress meters with dual-color energetic fills (`#4C1D95` to `#EC4899` gradient tracks).
- Integrated student avatar tokens rimmed with multi-color pastel rings denoting academic badges and extracurricular participation.
